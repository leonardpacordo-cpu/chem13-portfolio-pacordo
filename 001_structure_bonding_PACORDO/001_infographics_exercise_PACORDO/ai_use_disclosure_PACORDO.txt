The AI tools used for this activity were Perplexity AI and ChatGPT.
Perplexity AI was used to help identify credible scientific and government sources, organize information about the selected compounds, suggest infographic content, and prepare an APA-style reference list.
ChatGPT was used to help generate the infographic designs and visual layouts.
I remained responsible for selecting the compounds, reviewing the generated content, confirming the accuracy of the molecular structures and chemical information, double-checking the properties, uses, risks, and chemical-engineering connections, and verifying the references used in the infographics. The APA-format reference list submitted with this activity was prepared separately and may differ from the reference list suggested by the AI tools.
